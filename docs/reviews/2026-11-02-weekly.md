## Findings

### M1. Direct dependency `nodemailer` has high-severity advisories

`functions/package.json:21` pins `^9.0.1`; the audit lists 12 advisories fixed in 10.3.1
(addressparser DoS, SMTP reply-parsing DoS, a DNS-cache TLS `servername` reuse that can
disclose SMTP credentials across transports, and recipient-parsing bugs). Recipients are
member emails and the SMTP host is fixed, so exposure is low, but it is a direct dependency
on the email path of `notifyEventStatusChange`. The root `vite` and `firebase` highs are
dev-server or transitive. Spec: `docs/backlog/todo.deps-audit-nodemailer.md`.

checks. `CLAUDE.md` calls this doc the deep reference. Spec:
`docs/backlog/todo.data-flow-doc-sync.md`.
